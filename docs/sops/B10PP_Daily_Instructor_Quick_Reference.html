Step 4a
Linguistic Flexibility — Morning Debrief
1
Confirm submissions Check instructor dashboard. Follow up with non-submitters privately.
2
Select a 3/4 or 4/4 LF exemplar Instructor-selected (default) or volunteer at discretion. Identify the category assigned last night: WFF, SV, SC, or PI.
3
Read question and play response audio
  • 3a: Read the LF question aloud to the class
  • 3b: Play the selected student response audio
4
Discuss transcript and feedback — use your category below Display platform scaffold feedback. Ask: what did it identify? Does the class agree?
WFF — Word Form Flexibility
· Which word forms from the target family appeared?
· Were they used in grammatically correct contexts?
· How many different forms were deployed? (Target: 3+)
· Did any collocated phrases appear?
· What would make the variation more natural?
SV — Structural Variation
· Which target structure appeared? (relative clause / nominalization / passive / conditional)
· Was it formed correctly?
· Did it appear integrated into the argument, or feel appended?
· Did it add precision or meaningfully shift the argument?
· What would strengthen its accuracy or integration?
SC — Sentence Combining
· Which stems were used? Were they completed accurately and in context?
· Did the combined sentences flow naturally?
· Was the argument maintained and developed across the combined stems?
· Did the syntactic complexity add clarity, or create confusion?
· What would improve the sentence combining on the next attempt?
PI — Perspective Integration
· Which stakeholders appeared? Were their positions accurately represented?
· Which target connectors were used? (From the perspective of… / Proponents assume… / Critics contend…)
· Were underlying assumptions named explicitly?
· Did the response move between perspectives, or stay on one side?
· What would strengthen the perspective integration?
Step 4b
Linguistic Flexibility — Planning Session
1
Open tonight's LF question and scaffold Navigate to the Linguistic Flexibility section. Open the assigned question. Rotate through categories across the week: WFF (Word Form Flexibility) · SV (Structural Variation) · SC (Sentence Combining) · PI (Perspective Integration).
2
Model one move and assign Demonstrate one entry point using the scaffold. Students plan their response mentally. Students record at home tonight alongside their bundle.
⚠ Mon/Tue/Thu (full days) only. Wed/Fri half-days: skip LF, run CORE + ESO debrief only.
Step 5
Assign Tomorrow's Pre-Class Work
1
Assign the next bundle in sequence Navigate to the assignment panel on the instructor dashboard. Assign the next bundle (e.g. S1.2, S2.3). The policy audio, topic-seed audio, and domain-linked ESO question are pre-packaged.
2
Designate tonight's MTS State it by name and restate its core strategy in one sentence. Remind students: one listen, immediate spoken response, no notes.
3
Assign the Frames ESO question Follow the weekly arc:
  • Day 1: 1–2 most accessible questions · full scaffold
  • Day 2: 2 moderate questions · full scaffold
  • Day 3: 1–2 increasing difficulty · scaffold fading
  • Day 4: student selects 1–2 · cold production
  • Day 5: weekend bridge from general ESO bank · combo scaffold
4
Set expectations Inform students that a strong response from tonight's attempt will be played and discussed tomorrow.
