Step 1
CORE Bundle — Morning Debrief
1
Confirm submissions Check instructor dashboard. Follow up with non-submitters privately.
2
Memory Training Strategy (MTS) pair debrief
  • Restate last night's MTS in one sentence
  • Students pair for 3–4 minutes and discuss:
    • Did the strategy help retain the main idea?
    • Where did retrieval break down?
    • What would they do differently next time?
  • One pair briefs back to the class
3
Select a 3/4 or 4/4 exemplar Instructor-selected (default) or volunteer at discretion.
4
Play audio
4a
Play the policy CORE audio passage
4b
Play the selected student response audio
5
Discuss transcript and platform feedback
  • Was the main idea captured accurately?
  • Which details were retained? Which were missing?
  • What specific language made the response clear or precise?
  • Display platform scaffold feedback — what did it identify? Does the class agree?
  • Evaluate detail retention — was it strong? If yes, what made it land? If not, what would strengthen it?
  • What would the student need to do differently on the next attempt?
6
Connect to the score Reference the score on the dashboard. Name specifically what earned it.
↺ Repeat Steps 3–6 for the topic-seed CORE audio.
Note: Each nightly bundle contains two CORE listening passages — a policy audio and a topic-seed audio. Both are mandatory and scored identically. Both debrief the same way.

Step 3
ESO/Frames — Morning Debrief
1
Confirm submissions Check instructor dashboard. Follow up with non-submitters privately.
2
Select a 3/4 or 4/4 exemplar Instructor-selected (default) or volunteer at discretion.
3
Play audio
3a
Play the ESO question audio
3b
Play the selected student response audio
4
Discuss transcript and platform feedback
  • Which of the three frame steps are clearly present?
  • What specific language carried the argument at each step?
  • Display platform scaffold feedback — what did it identify? Does the class agree?
  • Evaluate the middle step — was it explicit and developed? If yes, what made it land? If not, what would strengthen it?
  • What would the student need to do differently on the next attempt?
4a
Optional repeat — additional response on same ESO question
  • Instructor selects or solicits a volunteer response
  • Play audio and display transcript
  • Focus on one comparative question: what did this response do differently at the middle step, or in spiral integration, compared to the first?
5
Address spiral integration (Week 2+)
  • Did prior frames appear?
  • Were they woven organically into the argument or appended?
  • If yes, what made the integration effective? If not, which prior frame would have strengthened the argument and where?

Step 4
Assign Tomorrow's Pre-Class Work
1
Assign the next bundle in sequence Navigate to the assignment panel on the instructor dashboard. Assign the next bundle (e.g. S1.2, S2.3). The policy audio, topic-seed audio, and domain-linked ESO question are pre-packaged.
2
Designate tonight's MTS State it by name and restate its core strategy in one sentence. Remind students: one listen, immediate spoken response, no notes.
3
Assign the Frames ESO question Follow the weekly arc:
  • Day 1: 1–2 most accessible questions · full scaffold
  • Day 2: 2 moderate questions · full scaffold
  • Day 3: 1–2 increasing difficulty · scaffold fading
  • Day 4: student selects 1–2 · cold production
  • Day 5: weekend bridge from general ESO bank · combo scaffold
4
Set expectations Inform students that a strong response from tonight's attempt will be played and discussed tomorrow.
