B10 Practice Platform · Tap a question to expand

About this frame: Project scenarios and trace what follows. The core move is the conditional: if X under condition Y, then Z would likely follow — and if Z holds, then... The Extended Scenario column is where ILR 2+/3 responses go further, chaining consequences and testing second-order assumptions.
ConditionThe specific circumstance that must hold
Projected OutcomeWhat would likely result if the condition holds
Extended ScenarioWhat follows from the outcome — second-order consequences
Key connectors: if… were implemented… · were… to… then… · should… fail to… · in a scenario where… · assuming… the effect would be… · if that outcome holds, then… · this in turn would lead to… · the second-order consequence would be… · provided that… · on the condition that…
Q1 Should higher education be made tuition-free at public universities?
EDU ▼
Condition
if funding is replaced through progressive taxation rather than flat levies
if quality standards are maintained through independent accreditation
if graduate and professional programs are included in the mandate
if private universities are not subject to the same requirement
Projected Outcome
enrollment increases across all income groups
credential inflation reduces the labor market premium of a degree
private universities gain selective advantage in prestige-driven markets
government education budget expands significantly
Extended Scenario
if credential inflation deepens, degree value declines for all graduates
if private universities gain prestige advantage, two-tier system entrenches class division
if quality falls with expanded enrollment, employers shift to alternative credential signals
if fiscal pressure mounts, funding is cut and quality deteriorates
Q2 Should gig economy workers receive the same benefits as full-time employees?
WRK ▼
Condition
if reclassification applies only above an income threshold
if platforms are given transition time to restructure their cost base
if flexibility is preserved through opt-out provisions for high-earning contractors
if enforcement is coordinated across jurisdictions to prevent regulatory arbitrage
Projected Outcome
lower-income gig workers gain healthcare and retirement access
platforms restructure pricing and workforce models to offset costs
high-earning contractors opt out, preserving flexibility for that segment
some platforms exit smaller or lower-margin markets
Extended Scenario
if platforms exit, gig work opportunities decrease in affected markets
if opt-out is widely used, protection gains are limited to lowest-income workers
if enforcement is uneven, platforms relocate operations to less regulated jurisdictions
if costs are passed to consumers, gig service prices rise with downstream demand effects
Q3 Should governments implement universal basic income?
ECN ▼
Condition
if UBI is funded through wealth and carbon taxes rather than income tax
if existing means-tested programs are maintained alongside UBI rather than replaced
if the UBI level is set at subsistence rather than replacement income
if implementation is phased with evidence review at each stage
Projected Outcome
poverty floor is raised without eliminating targeted support for high-need groups
entrepreneurial activity and risk-taking increase at lower income levels
tax base required is substantial and politically difficult to sustain
labor supply tightens modestly in low-wage sectors
Extended Scenario
if tax base is insufficient, UBI is funded through borrowing and inflation risk rises
if labor supply tightens, wages rise in low-wage sectors with downstream price effects
if targeted programs are maintained, administrative complexity and cost remain high
if phased evidence review reveals negative effects, political exit from UBI is costly
Q4 Should social media companies be required to remove political misinformation?
GOV ▼
Condition
if an independent non-governmental body defines misinformation standards
if removal applies only to demonstrably false factual claims, not contested opinions
if appeals processes are fast and transparent
if the same standards apply across all political affiliations equally
Projected Outcome
demonstrably false content decreases on regulated platforms
platforms invest heavily in moderation infrastructure to comply
contested political speech faces increased removal risk at the margins
false content migrates to unregulated platforms and offshore alternatives
Extended Scenario
if migration to unregulated platforms accelerates, regulated platforms lose the most engaged users
if contested speech is removed, legitimate political dissent is chilled
if appeals are slow, incorrect removals persist and trust in platforms decreases
if standards are applied unevenly, regulated platforms become politically captured
Q5 Should alternative medicine be covered by public health insurance?
HLT ▼
Condition
if coverage is limited to treatments with evidence of at least comparative effectiveness
if reimbursement rates are lower than conventional care equivalents
if coverage is conditioned on licensed practitioners only
if patient disclosure requirements prevent substitution for conventional care
Projected Outcome
patient access to preferred treatments broadens within evidence boundaries
practitioners with marginal evidence bases gain institutional legitimacy
insurance costs increase modestly with expanded coverage scope
some patients substitute alternative for conventional care despite disclosure requirements
Extended Scenario
if substitution occurs, delayed diagnosis increases downstream treatment costs
if legitimacy of marginal treatments grows, evidence standards are further eroded
if costs increase without outcome improvement, insurance fund sustainability is pressured
if coverage is well-designed, it reduces out-of-pocket spending without quality risk
Q6 Should governments regulate the development of artificial intelligence?
TEC ▼
Condition
if regulation is risk-tiered rather than applied uniformly to all AI systems
if regulatory sandboxes allow innovation to continue under supervised conditions
if international coordination prevents competitive regulatory arbitrage
if technical experts are embedded in regulatory bodies to enable meaningful oversight
Projected Outcome
high-risk AI deployment is slowed and made safer
low-risk AI innovation continues with minimal compliance burden
regulatory arbitrage drives some development to less regulated jurisdictions
public trust in AI increases as accountability mechanisms become visible
Extended Scenario
if arbitrage is significant, domestic firms are disadvantaged without global safety benefit
if sandboxes enable learning, regulation improves iteratively rather than locking in outdated rules
if technical capacity in regulators is insufficient, oversight is nominal
if trust increases, AI adoption accelerates with downstream productivity and efficiency gains
Q7 Should governments ban the development of new fossil fuel infrastructure?
ENV ▼
Condition
if the ban is accompanied by a just transition fund for affected workers and communities
if existing infrastructure is allowed to operate until end of economic life
if renewable energy capacity investment is mandated simultaneously
if the ban applies to domestic development but not to imports
Projected Outcome
new carbon lock-in from additional fossil fuel infrastructure is prevented
fossil fuel dependent communities face economic disruption without transition support
energy security risk increases during the transition period
carbon leakage occurs if production shifts to countries without the ban
Extended Scenario
if just transition support is inadequate, political backlash reverses the ban
if renewable investment lags, energy price spikes create economic and social instability
if carbon leakage is significant, global emissions are not reduced — only relocated
if ban succeeds with transition support, it becomes a model for other high-carbon sectors
Q8 Should governments implement stricter gun control measures?
JUS ▼
Condition
if measures target the highest-risk purchase pathways specifically
if red flag laws are accompanied by due process protections
if rural and urban contexts are treated differently in implementation
if buy-back programs reduce existing stock alongside purchase restrictions
Projected Outcome
legal purchase by high-risk individuals becomes significantly harder
mass casualty event lethality decreases with capacity restrictions
rural gun owners bear disproportionate compliance burden relative to urban risk profile
illegal firearms market expands to meet displaced legal demand
Extended Scenario
if illegal market expands, gun availability for criminals is not significantly reduced
if rural burden is disproportionate, political resistance makes enforcement impossible
if buy-back programs reduce stock, long-term reduction in gun-related homicide follows
if red flag laws lack due process, constitutional challenge succeeds and laws are struck down

How to Use These Phrases

Tap a question to expand its three columns. Pick one chunk from each column and connect them with conditional language.

"If CBDC is designed with strong privacy protections, the projected outcome is that financial inclusion improves — but if surveillance capacity grows alongside it, the extended scenario is that authoritarian misuse becomes structurally available, regardless of original intent."

Three chunks. Three connectors. A complete hypothetical argument with a second-order consequence. The Extended Scenario column is what separates an ILR 2+ response from an ILR 3 one — don't stop at the projected outcome.
