W2 — Trade-offs & Constraints · ESO Phrase Reference

B10 Practice Platform · Tap a question to expand

About this frame: Show what is gained, what is lost, and what structural limit prevents a cleaner solution. These chunks are building blocks — combine them with grammar stems to construct your own sentences.
BenefitWhat the policy achieves — who gains, what improves
Trade-offWhat is sacrificed — the competing good that is reduced or lost
ConstraintThe structural limit — why the trade-off cannot simply be avoided
Key connectors: although… nevertheless · at the expense of · while X, Y is sacrificed · the cost falls on · cannot simultaneously · this comes with the constraint that · a win-win outcome is unavailable because

How to Use These Phrases

Tap a question to expand its three columns. Pick one or two chunks from each column and connect them with trade-off language. For example:

"Although this policy removes the financial barrier for low-income students, it shifts the cost to taxpayers — and the constraint here is that fiscal sustainability varies significantly by state, making a uniform national mandate structurally difficult."

Three chunks. Two connectors. One ILR 2+/3 argument move. The Constraint column is what separates a basic trade-off observation from an ILR 3 response — don't stop at naming what is lost. Explain why the trade-off cannot simply be avoided.
