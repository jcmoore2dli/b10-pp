B10-PP Grammar Stems & Templates

How to use this reference: Part 1 covers universal grammar structures usable in any ESO response, any week. Part 2 covers the 2–3 grammar structures most salient for each week's discourse frame. Use stems as building blocks — combine them with content chunks from the weekly phrase reference sheets.

Part 1 — Universal Grammar Structures

These structures work across all six weeks. Master these first — they form the grammatical backbone of academic spoken argument at ILR 2+/3.

Universal — All Weeks ▼
Cross-Scale Connectors Universal ▼
Stems
At the individual level, ___ ; however, at the national level, ___.
While the immediate impact falls on ___, the longer-term consequences extend to ___.
This affects not only ___ at the community level, but also ___ at the scale of ___.
What begins as a concern for individual ___ quickly becomes a question of national ___.
From the perspective of ___, ___ ; yet when we consider ___ as a whole, ___.
Templates
At the individual level, workers face immediate job loss; however, at the national level, the long-term effect on tax revenue may be equally significant.
While the immediate impact falls on small business owners, the longer-term consequences extend to the communities that depend on them.
This affects not only students in mainstream classrooms, but also the broader institutional capacity of the school system as a whole.
Concession & Contrast Universal ▼
Stems
Although ___, ___.
While it may seem that ___, in fact ___.
Despite the fact that ___, ___.
Even though ___ benefits ___, it comes at the cost of ___.
___ ; nevertheless, ___.
On one hand, ___ ; on the other hand, ___.
Templates
Although cash transfers may provide short-term relief, the long-term inflationary effect cannot be ignored.
Despite the fact that this policy benefits large corporations, small businesses are disproportionately burdened.
This approach reduces costs for employers; nevertheless, the burden on working families remains unaddressed.
Modality & Hedging Universal ▼
Stems
This would likely ___.
There is a risk that ___.
It is possible that ___, though ___.
One might argue that ___.
This may ___, but it could also ___.
The evidence suggests that ___.
It is reasonable to assume that ___.
Templates
This policy would likely benefit higher-income households more than those in the lowest income bracket.
One might argue that direct citizen consultation strengthens democratic legitimacy, though it may also slow decision-making significantly.
It is reasonable to assume that without enforcement mechanisms, compliance rates would remain low.
Passive & Reporting Universal ▼
Stems
___ are disproportionately affected by ___.
___ are often overlooked in discussions of ___.
___ are left without access to ___.
It has been argued that ___.
It is widely recognized that ___.
Critics of this position argue that ___.
Proponents suggest that ___.
Templates
Low-income workers are disproportionately affected by automation policies that favor capital-intensive industries.
It has been argued that corporate tax harmonization is essential for fiscal fairness; however, critics point to sovereignty concerns.
Proponents suggest that early childhood investment yields long-term economic returns, though the timeline remains contested.
Nominalization Universal ▼
Stems
The displacement of ___
The burden on ___
The concentration of ___ among ___
The exclusion of ___ from ___
The dependence of ___ on ___
The tension between ___ and ___
The erosion of ___
Access to ___ for ___
Templates
The displacement of low-wage workers as a result of this policy creates pressure at both the family and community level.
The concentration of decision-making power among a small number of nations undermines the legitimacy of the global fund.
The tension between economic efficiency and social equity lies at the heart of this debate.

Part 2 — Week-by-Week Grammar Focus

Each week foregrounds 2–3 structures most salient to that frame's analytical demands. These are in addition to the universal structures above, not replacements for them.

W1 — Scale & Stakeholder ▼
Relative Clauses Primary ▼
Stems — Specifying Stakeholders
Those who ___
Workers who ___
Countries that ___
Communities that have ___
Individuals who depend on ___
Groups that are most directly affected by ___
Templates
Those who bear the greatest burden of this policy are low-income workers in export-dependent industries.
Countries that have historically contributed most to carbon emissions carry a greater moral obligation to fund climate adaptation.
Communities that rely on indigenous land rights stand to lose significant cultural and economic stability if this policy is implemented without consultation.
Nominalization Primary ▼
Stems — Compressing Stakeholder Conditions
The displacement of ___
The burden on ___
The exclusion of ___ from ___
The concentration of ___ among ___
Access to ___ for ___
Templates
The displacement of low-wage workers as a direct consequence of automation creates pressure at both the family and community level.
The burden on small business owners is disproportionate when mandates are applied uniformly regardless of company size.
The exclusion of indigenous communities from policy consultation undermines both the legitimacy and the effectiveness of the decision.
Passive & Reporting Tertiary ▼
Stems — Foregrounding Impact
___ are disproportionately impacted by ___
___ are frequently left without ___
___ are often overlooked in ___
Templates
Elderly residents in rural areas are frequently left without adequate access to mental health services under current funding structures.
Part-time workers are often overlooked in childcare benefit discussions that focus primarily on full-time employment contracts.
W2 — Trade-offs & Constraints ▼
Concession & Contrast Primary ▼
Stems — Weighing Competing Goods
Although ___ offers ___, it comes at the cost of ___.
While ___ benefits ___, the trade-off for ___ is ___.
The advantage of ___ must be weighed against ___.
___ ; however, this comes with the constraint that ___.
Even if ___, the risk to ___ remains significant.
Templates
Although a global minimum corporate tax reduces profit-shifting, it comes at the cost of fiscal autonomy for smaller economies.
While mandatory childcare provision benefits working parents, the trade-off for small businesses operating on narrow margins is substantial.
The advantage of direct citizen consultation must be weighed against the risk of decision paralysis on time-sensitive policy questions.
Modality & Hedging Primary ▼
Stems — Projecting Constrained Outcomes
If ___ is prioritized, then ___ may be compromised.
The more ___, the less likely it is that ___.
There is a risk that prioritizing ___ will come at the expense of ___.
It is unlikely that both ___ and ___ can be fully achieved simultaneously.
Templates
If cost efficiency is prioritized in healthcare funding, it is unlikely that equity of access can be fully maintained simultaneously.
There is a risk that prioritizing economic growth will come at the expense of environmental sustainability in the short term.
Conditional Structures Tertiary ▼
Stems — Projecting Constrained Scenarios
If resources are limited, then prioritizing ___ means sacrificing ___.
Were ___ to be implemented without ___, the result would likely be ___.
Templates
If healthcare resources are limited, then prioritizing mental health parity means reallocating funds currently designated for physical infrastructure.
W3 — Causal Systems ▼
Nominalization Primary ▼
Stems — Packaging Causes and Effects
The accumulation of ___
The erosion of ___
The amplification of ___ through ___
The suppression of ___ as a result of ___
The collapse of ___ driven by ___
The reinforcement of ___ by ___
Templates
The accumulation of sovereign debt driven by stimulus spending creates a secondary constraint on post-crisis recovery.
The erosion of consumer confidence during periods of high inflation suppresses discretionary spending across income groups.
The amplification of inequality through automation-driven displacement represents a systemic risk, not merely an individual one.
Passive & Reporting Primary ▼
Stems — Foregrounding Effects Over Agents
___ is driven by ___
___ is suppressed when ___
___ is amplified by ___
___ is undermined by ___
___ is reinforced through ___
As ___ increases, ___ is put under pressure.
Templates
Inflation is driven not by the initial stimulus but by the mismatch between spending patterns and productive capacity during recovery.
Wage growth is suppressed when labor market conditions favor employers in sectors where automation has reduced bargaining power.
Public trust in institutions is undermined when policy decisions are made without transparent consultation processes.
Relative Clauses Tertiary ▼
Stems — Specifying Causal Conditions
A system that relies on ___
Policies that fail to account for ___
Mechanisms that reinforce ___
Templates
A system that relies on consumer spending as its primary recovery driver is vulnerable to inflationary pressure during supply-constrained periods.
W4 — Hypothetical & Conditional ▼
Conditional Structures Primary ▼
Stems — Projecting Scenarios
If ___ were implemented, the most likely outcome would be ___.
Were ___ to ___, then ___.
If this policy were adopted without ___, it would ___.
Should ___ fail to ___, the consequence would be ___.
In a scenario where ___, ___ would likely ___.
Assuming ___, the effect on ___ would be ___.
Templates
If a global minimum corporate tax were implemented without a unified enforcement mechanism, compliance would likely remain voluntary and inconsistent.
Were governments to consult citizens directly on every major policy decision, the risk of decision paralysis on time-sensitive issues would increase significantly.
In a scenario where fossil fuel subsidies are redirected to the global climate fund, developing nations would gain access to resources currently unavailable to them.
Modality & Hedging Primary ▼
Stems — Calibrating Projection Certainty
This would likely ___, though it might also ___.
The probable outcome is ___, with the caveat that ___.
It is reasonable to project that ___.
There is a strong likelihood that ___, provided that ___.
One could argue that ___, but it is equally possible that ___.
Templates
This policy would likely reduce offshore profit-shifting in the short term, though it might also incentivize structural reorganization to avoid compliance.
There is a strong likelihood that early investment in childcare infrastructure would yield long-term workforce participation gains, provided that quality standards are maintained.
W5 — Values, Heuristics & Bias ▼
Concession & Contrast Primary ▼
Stems — Surfacing Competing Value Frameworks
While some prioritize ___, others argue that ___.
From a ___ perspective, ___ ; however, from a ___ perspective, ___.
Those who value ___ tend to conclude that ___, whereas those who prioritize ___ reach a different conclusion.
The tension here is not primarily empirical but normative — it depends on whether one values ___ or ___.
Although ___ appears to be a neutral position, it implicitly prioritizes ___ over ___.
Templates
While some prioritize economic efficiency in healthcare allocation, others argue that equity of access is a non-negotiable baseline, regardless of cost.
The tension here is not primarily empirical but normative — it depends on whether one values individual economic freedom or collective environmental responsibility.
Although a market-based approach appears neutral, it implicitly prioritizes outcomes that favor established economic actors over emerging or vulnerable ones.
Modality & Hedging Primary ▼
Stems — Marking Value Claims as Perspective-Dependent
One might argue that ___, though this depends on whether ___.
This position assumes that ___, which not everyone would accept.
The validity of this claim rests on the assumption that ___.
Whether ___ is seen as ___ or ___ depends largely on ___.
It is worth noting that ___ reflects a particular value framework, namely ___.
Templates
One might argue that governments have an obligation to fund indigenous cultural preservation, though this depends on whether cultural continuity is treated as a public good.
This position assumes that individual economic freedom outweighs collective social benefit, which not everyone would accept as a starting premise.
Whether mandatory childcare provision is seen as a benefit or a burden depends largely on the size and financial structure of the employer in question.
Passive & Reporting Tertiary ▼
Stems — Attributing Value Positions to Stakeholders
It is widely held that ___.
Critics of this view argue that ___.
Proponents of ___ tend to assume that ___.
Templates
It is widely held that economic growth and environmental protection are fundamentally in tension, though this framing has been challenged by proponents of sustainable development.
W6 — Synthesis & Judgment ▼
Nominalization Primary ▼
Stems — Compressing Prior Arguments for Synthesis
Given the tension between ___ and ___
Weighing ___ against ___
Taking into account both ___ and ___
In light of the competing considerations of ___ and ___
The cumulative effect of ___, ___, and ___ suggests that ___
Balancing the interests of ___ with those of ___
Templates
Given the tension between fiscal sovereignty and global equity, a tiered contribution model appears more sustainable than a uniform mandate.
Weighing the short-term cost burden on small businesses against the long-term workforce benefits, the case for subsidized rather than mandatory childcare is stronger.
Taking into account both the historical emissions record of developed nations and the adaptive capacity of developing ones, differentiated obligations are more defensible than equal ones.
Concession & Contrast Primary ▼
Stems — The Final Weighing Move
While both positions have merit, the stronger case rests on ___.
Although ___ is a legitimate concern, it does not outweigh ___.
The evidence on both sides is significant; however, ___ ultimately carries more weight because ___.
Neither position is without weakness, but ___ is more defensible because ___.
On balance, ___ because ___.
Templates
While both positions have merit, the stronger case rests on the long-term fiscal and social benefits of mental health parity rather than on short-term cost projections.
Although sovereignty concerns are legitimate, they do not outweigh the collective obligation to address a global environmental crisis with asymmetric impacts.
On balance, a graduated global minimum tax is more defensible than a uniform rate, because it accounts for differences in national economic capacity without abandoning the principle of fairness.
Modality & Hedging Tertiary ▼
Stems — Calibrating the Final Judgment
On balance, ___.
The evidence suggests that ___.
While certainty is not possible, the most defensible position is ___.
It would be premature to conclude that ___, but the available evidence points toward ___.
Templates
On balance, the long-term social and economic benefits of universal childcare access outweigh the short-term compliance costs for mid-size employers.
While certainty is not possible given the complexity of global tax enforcement, the most defensible position is that a minimum floor is preferable to no standard at all.

A Final Reminder

Grammar structures are tools. A stem used without specific, precise content — named stakeholders, concrete consequences, clearly stated positions — does not demonstrate ILR 2+/3 performance. The scorer reads for analytical depth, not for the presence of particular structures. Use these templates to build the habit, then let the argument drive the language.
