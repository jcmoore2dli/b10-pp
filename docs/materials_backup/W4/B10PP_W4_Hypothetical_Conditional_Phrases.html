W4 — Hypothetical & Conditional · ESO Phrase Reference

B10 Practice Platform · Tap a question to expand

About this frame: Project scenarios and trace what follows. The core move is the conditional: if X under condition Y, then Z would likely follow — and if Z holds, then... The Extended Scenario column is where ILR 2+/3 responses go further than a basic conditional, chaining consequences and testing assumptions.
Condition The specific circumstance that must hold
Projected Outcome What would likely result if the condition holds
Extended Scenario What follows from the outcome — second-order consequences
Key connectors: if… were implemented… · were… to… then… · should… fail to… · in a scenario where… · assuming… the effect would be… · if that outcome holds, then… · this in turn would lead to… · the second-order consequence would be… · provided that… · on the condition that…
Q1Should AI teachers replace human instructors in some schools?
EDU▼
Condition
if AI deployment is limited to underserved regions
if human instructors remain in a supervisory role
if AI quality meets minimum pedagogical standards
if infrastructure and connectivity are guaranteed
Projected Outcome
access to consistent instruction expands in under-resourced areas
teacher shortage in rural and remote contexts is partially addressed
social-emotional development gaps emerge without human presence
equity of AI quality becomes a new axis of educational inequality
Extended Scenario
if quality gaps persist, AI adoption widens rather than closes the achievement divide
if social-emotional gaps compound, long-term workforce readiness declines
if successful, pressure mounts to expand AI replacement beyond initial scope
if teacher profession declines, human instructional capacity becomes harder to rebuild
Q2Should personalized learning algorithms be mandatory in public education?
EDU▼
Condition
if algorithm quality is independently verified before deployment
if student data is protected under strict privacy law
if teachers retain authority to override algorithmic recommendations
if access to quality algorithms is equitably distributed
Projected Outcome
struggling students receive more targeted content delivery
student data accumulates at scale creating systemic privacy risk
teacher role shifts from instruction toward facilitation
vendor dependency becomes embedded in public education infrastructure
Extended Scenario
if vendor dependency deepens, curriculum control shifts away from public institutions
if privacy protections fail, student profiles become commercially exploitable
if teacher capacity declines, human judgment in education is structurally eroded
if outcomes improve selectively, mandatory adoption entrenches unequal benefit
Q3Should cryptocurrency be regulated as a financial instrument?
ECN▼
Condition
if regulation is coordinated across major jurisdictions simultaneously
if compliance thresholds accommodate smaller retail participants
if enforcement targets exchanges rather than individual holders
if unbanked populations retain alternative access pathways
Projected Outcome
institutional investment increases with regulatory clarity
money laundering via crypto becomes significantly harder
market consolidates around large compliant exchanges
unregulated crypto activity migrates to offshore platforms
Extended Scenario
if consolidation continues, crypto market resembles traditional finance with same systemic risks
if offshore migration grows, regulatory benefit is captured by non-signatory jurisdictions
if institutional adoption accelerates, crypto volatility decreases but decentralization is lost
if unbanked access is blocked, financial inclusion benefits of crypto are eliminated
Q4Should central banks adopt digital currencies?
ECN▼
Condition
if CBDC is designed with strong privacy protections
if commercial banks retain a role in CBDC distribution
if programmable features are limited to specific use cases
if interoperability with existing payment systems is guaranteed
Projected Outcome
financial inclusion improves for unbanked citizens
monetary policy transmission becomes more direct and precise
commercial bank deposit base declines as citizens hold CBDC directly
government surveillance capacity over individual transactions increases
Extended Scenario
if bank deposit base declines, credit availability tightens with downstream economic effects
if surveillance capacity grows, authoritarian misuse becomes structurally available
if programmable features expand, government control over spending patterns follows
if public trust is lost, CBDC adoption fails and investment is wasted
Q5Should governments implement a digital identity system for all citizens?
GOV▼
Condition
if enrollment is voluntary rather than mandatory
if data use is strictly limited to service delivery
if alternative non-digital access pathways are maintained
if independent oversight of the system is legally guaranteed
Projected Outcome
government service delivery efficiency improves for enrolled citizens
identity fraud decreases significantly
citizens without documentation face deeper exclusion
surveillance infrastructure is built alongside service delivery
Extended Scenario
if surveillance infrastructure expands, scope creep toward social control becomes structurally available
if exclusion deepens, marginalized populations lose access to essential services
if voluntary system succeeds, pressure to make it mandatory increases
if data breach occurs, biometric data cannot be changed — harm is permanent
Q6Should governments fund research into life extension technologies?
HLT▼
Condition
if breakthroughs are made available as global public goods
if pension and retirement systems are reformed in parallel
if research is focused on healthspan rather than lifespan alone
if ethical oversight governs distribution and access
Projected Outcome
healthy productive lifespan extends for populations with access
age-related disease burden decreases in the long term
pension and retirement systems face structural insolvency
global inequality deepens if access is limited to wealthy nations
Extended Scenario
if pension systems are not reformed, fiscal crisis follows extended lifespan
if access is unequal, life extension becomes the ultimate marker of global inequality
if healthspan improves without lifespan extension, workforce participation and productivity increase
if population growth accelerates, resource and environmental pressure compounds
Q7Should there be a global moratorium on autonomous weapons development?
TEC▼
Condition
if all major military powers sign and ratify the moratorium
if verification mechanisms are technically feasible
if defensive autonomous systems are distinguished from offensive ones
if non-signatory states face meaningful consequences for non-compliance
Projected Outcome
autonomous weapons proliferation to non-state actors is slowed
human accountability in use of force is maintained
nations outside the moratorium gain asymmetric military advantage
defense AI investment is redirected to non-lethal applications
Extended Scenario
if non-signatories gain advantage, signatory nations face pressure to withdraw
if verification fails, moratorium becomes unenforceable and collapses
if successful, precedent for AI weapons governance is established for future technologies
if defensive systems are banned, vulnerability of signatory nations increases
Q8Should there be a global ban on nuclear weapons?
INT▼
Condition
if all nuclear-armed states ratify and comply
if verification regime is technically robust
if conventional arms buildup is addressed simultaneously
if breakout risk is managed through inspection access
Projected Outcome
existential risk of nuclear exchange decreases among signatories
mutual assured destruction framework collapses
conventional arms competition intensifies to fill deterrence gap
non-nuclear states gain strategic parity with former nuclear powers
Extended Scenario
if conventional arms race follows, conflict frequency may increase despite nuclear risk reduction
if one state secretly retains weapons, it gains decisive asymmetric advantage
if verification fails, ban collapses and trust between nuclear states is permanently damaged
if successful, basis of great power competition shifts to conventional and cyber domains

How to Use These Phrases

Tap a question to expand its three columns. Pick one chunk from each column and connect them with conditional language. For example:

"If CBDC is designed with strong privacy protections, the projected outcome is that financial inclusion improves for unbanked citizens — but if surveillance capacity grows alongside it, the extended scenario is that authoritarian misuse becomes structurally available, regardless of original intent."

Three chunks. Three connectors. A complete hypothetical argument with a second-order consequence. The Extended Scenario column is what separates an ILR 2+ response from an ILR 3 one — don't stop at the projected outcome.
