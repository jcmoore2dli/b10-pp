Q1 Should higher education be made tuition-free at public universities?
EDU ▼
Trigger
tuition elimination at public universities enacted
government funding replaces tuition revenue
access barrier removed for low-income students
universal enrollment eligibility established
Mechanism
enrollment increases significantly across income groups
government education budget expands to cover lost tuition
private universities face enrollment and revenue pressure
credential value shifts as degree becomes more universal
Downstream Effect
higher-skilled workforce emerges over medium term
credential inflation reduces return on degree investment
fiscal pressure on government education budgets increases
long-term reduction in income inequality linked to education access
Q2 Should gig economy workers receive the same benefits as full-time employees?
WRK ▼
Trigger
gig worker reclassification as employees enacted
benefit parity requirement applied to platform workers
employment law extended to cover gig labor
mandatory benefits floor set for all workers
Mechanism
platform operating costs rise significantly
gig worker income stability improves for those retained
some platforms automate or reduce workforce to offset costs
traditional employment becomes more competitive alternative
Downstream Effect
gig labor market contracts in regulated jurisdictions
informal labor market expands as displaced workers seek alternatives
long-term improvement in retirement and health security for gig workers
platform business model disruption accelerates automation investment
Q3 Should governments implement universal basic income?
ECN ▼
Trigger
universal basic income enacted at national level
unconditional cash transfer to all adult citizens
existing means-tested programs partially replaced
UBI funded through taxation or reallocation
Mechanism
consumer spending increases at lower income levels
labor market participation shifts at margin
social service administrative complexity reduces
entrepreneurial activity increases as failure risk is cushioned
Downstream Effect
poverty rate decreases at income floor level
labor supply tightens in low-wage sectors over time
inflationary pressure emerges if UBI is money-creation funded
long-term shift in relationship between work and income security
Q4 Should social media companies be required to remove political misinformation?
GOV ▼
Trigger
mandatory misinformation removal requirement enacted
platform liability for false political content established
algorithmic demotion of flagged content required
government or independent body defines misinformation threshold
Mechanism
platforms invest in content moderation infrastructure
false content removal rate increases but error rate also rises
political actors adapt by using coded or indirect language
smaller platforms struggle to meet compliance requirements
Downstream Effect
misinformation volume decreases on regulated platforms
false content migrates to unregulated or offshore platforms
public trust in regulated platforms increases modestly
long-term shift in where political misinformation circulates
Q5 Should alternative medicine be covered by public health insurance?
HLT ▼
Trigger
alternative medicine coverage added to public insurance
reimbursement framework extended to non-conventional treatments
patient choice in treatment modality legally recognized
coverage threshold defined by regulatory body
Mechanism
demand for alternative treatments increases as cost barrier falls
conventional providers face reimbursement competition
some patients substitute alternative for conventional care
insurance fund costs increase without clear outcome improvement
Downstream Effect
patient access to preferred treatments broadens
evidence-based resource allocation is diluted
long-term quality outcome data remains mixed
conventional care delayed in some patient populations downstream
Q6 Should governments regulate the development of artificial intelligence?
TEC ▼
Trigger
AI regulation framework enacted with compliance requirements
high-risk AI systems subject to pre-deployment assessment
algorithmic accountability standards legally mandated
AI development licensing or registration required
Mechanism
AI companies invest in compliance and safety infrastructure
development timelines lengthen for regulated system categories
smaller firms face disproportionate compliance burden
regulatory arbitrage drives some development to less regulated jurisdictions
Downstream Effect
harmful AI deployment rate decreases in regulated markets
innovation pace slows modestly in high-compliance categories
public trust in AI systems increases over medium term
long-term competitive asymmetry between regulated and unregulated markets

ENV ▼
Trigger
ban on new fossil fuel infrastructure development enacted
fossil fuel investment halted by regulatory prohibition
no new extraction, pipeline, or refinery permits issued
existing infrastructure allowed to operate until end of life
Mechanism
energy investment shifts toward renewable alternatives
fossil fuel industry employment declines in affected regions
energy transition accelerates in markets with viable alternatives
energy security risk increases during transition period
Downstream Effect
carbon emissions trajectory declines over medium to long term
fossil fuel dependent communities face structural economic disruption
renewable energy capacity expands to fill investment gap
long-term energy independence from fossil fuel imports increases
Q8 Should governments implement stricter gun control measures?
JUS ▼
Trigger
stricter gun control legislation enacted
background check requirements expanded
high-capacity magazine and assault weapon restrictions imposed
waiting periods and red flag laws implemented
Mechanism
legal firearm acquisition becomes more difficult and time-consuming
some high-risk individuals are screened out of legal purchase
illegal firearms market remains largely unaffected
law enforcement faces new compliance monitoring responsibilities
Downstream Effect
gun death rate decreases modestly in jurisdictions with strong enforcement
mass casualty event lethality decreases with capacity restrictions
illegal firearm trafficking increases to meet displaced demand
long-term cultural shift in relationship to firearms in some communities

How to Use These Phrases

Tap a question to expand its three columns. Pick one chunk from each column and connect them with causal language.

"Mandatory curriculum integration triggers a process in which students develop habitual source-checking behavior — and the downstream effect, over a generation, is a population significantly more resistant to foreign disinformation campaigns."

Three chunks. Two connectors. One complete causal argument. Do not skip the Mechanism column. Going straight from trigger to effect is the most common weakness in Causal Systems responses.
