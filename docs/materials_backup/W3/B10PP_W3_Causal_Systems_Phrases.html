Key connectors: this triggers… · as a direct result of… · the mechanism here is… · this in turn leads to… · the downstream consequence is… · what drives this effect is… · the causal chain runs from… to… · this amplifies / suppresses / accelerates…
Q1 Should schools integrate media literacy education into their curricula?
EDU ▼
Trigger
mandatory curriculum integration
structured source evaluation practice
systematic misinformation exposure training
critical reading skills embedded in instruction
Mechanism
students develop habitual source checking
false content recognition improves over time
reduced emotional susceptibility to viral content
critical evaluation becomes automatic
Downstream Effect
lower misinformation spread at population level
stronger informed citizenry over a generation
reduced effectiveness of disinformation campaigns
healthier democratic discourse downstream
Q2 Should the gig economy be more strictly regulated?
WRK ▼
Trigger
reclassification of gig workers as employees
mandatory benefit provision by platforms
stricter labor law enforcement in gig sector
minimum wage floor applied to gig earnings
Mechanism
platform operating costs rise significantly
some platforms reduce workforce or exit markets
remaining workers gain benefits but lose flexibility
traditional employers face reduced cost advantage
Downstream Effect
gig labor market contracts in regulated jurisdictions
worker protection improves for those who remain
informal labor market expands as platforms exit
long-term shift in contingent work structure

How to Use These Phrases

Tap a question to expand its three columns. Pick one chunk from each column and connect them with causal language. For example:

"Mandatory curriculum integration triggers a process in which students develop habitual source-checking behavior — and the downstream effect, over a generation, is a population significantly more resistant to foreign disinformation campaigns."

Three chunks. Two connectors. One complete causal argument. Do not skip the Mechanism column. Going straight from trigger to effect is the most common weakness in Causal Systems responses.
