Q1 Should students with disabilities always be fully included in mainstream classrooms?
EDU ▼
Trigger
mandatory full inclusion policy enacted
removal of separate special education placement
legal requirement for mainstream access
disability rights framework applied to placement
Mechanism
schools must redesign classroom support structures
teachers adapt instruction for wider ability range
specialist staff are redeployed into mainstream settings
resource allocation shifts from pull-out to in-class support
Downstream Effect
inclusion outcomes vary significantly by support quality
social integration improves for students with disabilities
teacher workload and stress increase without adequate resourcing
long-term normalization of disability in educational settings

Trigger

corporate childcare subsidy mandated by regulation
on-site or subsidized childcare legally required
Mechanism

workforce retention improves among working parents
gender gap in workforce participation narrows
childcare industry restructures around employer partnerships
Downstream Effect
higher female workforce participation over medium term
small business compliance burden creates market pressure
long-term reduction in gender pay gap downstream
childcare quality becomes linked to employer financial capacity
Q3 Should countries adopt a global minimum corporate tax?

Trigger

Mechanism

race-to-the-bottom tax competition is slowed

long-term shift in where multinational profits are reported

parity requirement embedded in national health policy
Mechanism

Q6 Should tech companies be required to pay taxes in every country they operate?
TEC ▼
Trigger
digital services tax applied in each country of operation
physical presence rule replaced by user-base tax threshold
OECD Pillar One framework enacted across jurisdictions
tax liability linked to revenue earned in each market
Mechanism
tech companies restructure operations and pricing models
governments in developing nations gain access to new tax base
compliance costs increase significantly for global platforms
transfer pricing strategies become less effective
Downstream Effect
tax revenue redistributed toward countries of user activity
developing nations gain fiscal capacity from digital economy
tech platform profitability decreases in higher-tax markets
long-term shift in where digital economy value is taxed

How to Use These Phrases

Tap a question to expand its three columns. Pick one chunk from each column and connect them with causal language.

"Mandatory curriculum integration triggers a process in which students develop habitual source-checking behavior — and the downstream effect, over a generation, is a population significantly more resistant to foreign disinformation campaigns."

Three chunks. Two connectors. One complete causal argument. Do not skip the Mechanism column. Going straight from trigger to effect is the most common weakness in Causal Systems responses.
