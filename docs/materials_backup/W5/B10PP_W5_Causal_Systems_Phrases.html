Q1 Should universities limit legacy admissions?
EDU ▼
Trigger
legacy admissions preference eliminated at universities
admissions criteria shifted to merit and need only
alumni donation influence on admissions formally removed
blind admissions process for legacy-eligible applicants enacted
Mechanism
admissions pool composition shifts toward first-generation students
alumni donation motivation partially decoupled from admissions benefit
university fundraising model faces pressure to adapt
meritocratic admissions narrative is strengthened institutionally
Downstream Effect
socioeconomic diversity of student population increases over time
university fundraising revenue decreases in short to medium term
upstream preparation inequality continues to shape who competes
long-term shift in who gains access to elite institutional networks
Q2 Should companies be required to disclose pay scales for all positions?
WRK ▼
Trigger
mandatory pay scale disclosure enacted for all positions
salary band transparency required in job postings and internal systems
pay equity reporting extended to include position-level data
employees gain legal right to access pay scale information
Mechanism
wage-setting becomes visible and comparable across employees
discriminatory pay gaps become harder to sustain and easier to challenge
internal salary tension increases among employees at different levels
employers standardize compensation structures to reduce disclosure risk
Downstream Effect
gender and racial pay gaps narrow in organizations with enforcement
employee satisfaction with pay equity improves where gaps close
high earners may seek employers with less transparent structures
long-term shift toward market-visible compensation norms
Q3 Should inheritance taxes be significantly increased?
ECN ▼
Trigger
inheritance tax rate significantly increased on large estates
estate tax threshold lowered to capture more transfers
intergenerational wealth transfer subject to higher levy
gifting and trust loopholes subject to additional scrutiny
Mechanism
wealthy families accelerate gifting and trust restructuring
estate planning industry expands to advise on avoidance strategies
government revenue from estate taxes increases for compliant transfers
family business and farm transfers face liquidity challenges
Downstream Effect
intergenerational wealth concentration slows at margin
tax avoidance through complex structures increases
public revenue available for equality-promoting investment rises
long-term reduction in dynastic wealth accumulation at top of distribution
Q4 Should there be stricter limits on political donations from corporations?
GOV ▼
Trigger
strict caps on corporate political donations enacted
corporate political expenditure subject to disclosure requirements
independent expenditure by corporations limited by law
dark money channels through non-profits subject to new restrictions
Mechanism
corporate political influence through direct donation is reduced
political parties seek alternative funding sources
dark money flows adapt to new restrictions through legal workarounds
public interest advocacy groups gain relative financial standing
Downstream Effect
legislative outcomes become modestly less skewed toward donor interests
corporate influence shifts toward lobbying rather than direct donation
political funding transparency increases where disclosure is enforced
long-term reduction in perceived corporate capture of electoral process
Q5 Should euthanasia be legalized in all countries?
HLT ▼
Trigger
euthanasia legalized with defined eligibility and safeguard criteria
medical aid in dying available upon request for eligible patients
legal framework for voluntary death enacted at national level
physician participation made voluntary within legal framework
Mechanism
terminally ill patients gain access to controlled end-of-life option
palliative care investment faces reallocation pressure
medical profession navigates tension between healing and end-of-life roles
vulnerable populations face increased pressure from family or institutions
Downstream Effect
patient autonomy at end of life is legally recognized
eligibility criteria expand over time in some jurisdictions
palliative care quality decreases if investment is redirected
long-term cultural shift in relationship to death and medical intervention
Q6 Should governments regulate the collection and use of biometric data?
TEC ▼
Trigger
biometric data collection subject to consent and purpose limitation
facial recognition use in public spaces restricted or banned
biometric database retention limits legally mandated
algorithmic bias in biometric systems subject to audit requirement
Mechanism
biometric surveillance infrastructure expansion is constrained
law enforcement loses access to real-time facial recognition tools
tech companies redesign products to meet consent requirements
minority communities face reduced exposure to biased algorithmic systems
Downstream Effect
civil liberties protection in digital public space is strengthened
law enforcement effectiveness decreases in some surveillance-dependent contexts
public trust in government biometric systems increases with regulation
long-term reduction in discriminatory outcomes from biometric misidentification
Q7 Should private prisons be banned?
JUS ▼
Trigger
private prison contracts prohibited by legislation
existing private facilities transferred to public operation
profit motive removed from incarceration management
government assumes full operational responsibility for corrections
Mechanism
private prison companies exit corrections market
government absorbs capacity at significant short-term cost
rural communities dependent on prison employment face economic disruption
rehabilitation program investment shifts under public management
Downstream Effect
incarceration conditions improve where public standards are higher
structural incentive to maintain high incarceration rates is reduced
government corrections budget increases significantly
long-term improvement in recidivism rates if rehabilitation investment follows
Q8 Should there be stricter international regulations on the use of private military companies?
INT ▼
Trigger
binding international framework for PMC operations enacted
PMC licensing and accountability standards legally mandated
use of PMCs in active conflict zones subject to international law
state liability for PMC actions established under international framework
Mechanism
PMC operators face increased legal exposure for civilian harm
states reduce use of PMCs to avoid accountability implications
PMC industry consolidates around compliant larger operators
proxy conflict via PMCs becomes legally and politically more costly
Downstream Effect
accountability gap for civilian harm in conflict narrows
major powers adapt to use PMCs in less regulated jurisdictions
international humanitarian law coverage of non-state armed actors expands
long-term reduction in plausible deniability for state-sponsored PMC operations

How to Use These Phrases

Tap a question to expand its three columns. Pick one chunk from each column and connect them with causal language.

"Mandatory curriculum integration triggers a process in which students develop habitual source-checking behavior — and the downstream effect, over a generation, is a population significantly more resistant to foreign disinformation campaigns."

Three chunks. Two connectors. One complete causal argument. Do not skip the Mechanism column. Going straight from trigger to effect is the most common weakness in Causal Systems responses.
