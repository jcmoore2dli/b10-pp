Frames Practice — Argument Builder

B10 Practice Platform · Use before you record · Ask each question in sequence

How to use this sheet: Before you record, identify your frame for this passage. Work through the three questions in order — left to right. Your response needs to answer all three. A response that skips the middle question is incomplete.
W1 Scale & Stakeholder
Stakeholder Who is affected and at what level?
Impact What specifically happens to them?
Scale Consideration Why does operating at that level change the argument?
W2 Trade-offs & Constraints
Benefit What does the policy achieve?
Trade-off What competing good is sacrificed?
Constraint Why can't the trade-off simply be avoided?
W3 Causal Systems
Trigger What sets the causal chain in motion?
Mechanism How does the effect actually get produced?
Downstream Effect What results at the end of the chain?
W4 Hypothetical & Conditional
Condition What condition must hold for this scenario to follow?
Projected Outcome What would likely result?
Extended Scenario And if that outcome holds, then what?
W5 Values, Heuristics & Bias
Value What does this stakeholder believe is important?
Assumption What must be true for that value to justify their position?
Competing Framework What does a stakeholder on the other side believe instead?
W6 Synthesis & Judgment
Tensions What competing claims must be addressed?
Priorities Which consideration carries more weight and why?
Judgment What is the most defensible conclusion?
Remember: The middle column is where most responses fall short. Naming a trade-off, a mechanism, a projected outcome, an assumption, or a priority — and explaining why — is what separates an ILR 2+ response from an ILR 3 one.
