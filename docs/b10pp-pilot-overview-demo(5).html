Section 2 — The Student Practice Loop

1
Student signs in with email and password. No personal name, rank, or identifier is stored — only a B10 ID number.
2
Assigned passages appear. For audio passages, the student listens first. A Speaking Focus cue appears if a scaffold is active.
3
Student records their response. One tap to start, one tap to stop. Works on iOS and PC — no app download required.
4
AI pipeline processes the submission. Transcription → disfluency analysis → scoring → structured feedback. Typical turnaround: under 30 seconds.
5
Student reviews feedback — score, strengths, gaps, and actionable language notes.

Section 3 — The Instructor View

Assign
Select any passage or bundle from the full corpus. Filter by layer or assign a pre-built bundle of 3 passages at once.
Scaffold
For ESO passages, optionally set a speaking focus — Argument Structure, Discourse Frame, or Grammar Structure. Score is never affected.
Monitor
Assignment history shows Pending / Attempted status per passage. Tap any attempt to expand: transcript, strengths, gaps, and language feedback.
Roster
Add any student by B10 ID. No administrator needed. A student working with multiple instructors simultaneously is fully supported.

Section 4 — How the AI Pipeline Works

Record
Student records in browser. Audio uploaded to secure storage.
→
Transcribe
Speech-to-text with word-level timestamps returned.
→
Analyze
Disfluency pre-processor runs on timestamp data.
→
Score
AI scores transcript against task type and scaffold.
→
Feedback
Structured feedback written to database. Student sees results.

On disfluency and fairness. The speech-to-text engine returns word-level timestamps for every token in the transcript. These are used to compute a disfluency profile — false starts, repetitions, filled pauses — before the transcript reaches the scoring engine. This protects students from false penalties caused by transcription error rather than actual performance.

Section 6 — Security by Design

B10 ID Pseudonyms
Students are identified only by a B10 ID number. No real name or personal identifier is collected, stored, or transmitted at any point.
Authenticated Access
All users sign in with email and password. Student accounts contain no personally identifiable information beyond the B10 ID.
Role-Based Data Access
Instructors see only their own students' data. Access is enforced at the database level — not just in the interface.
Audio Deleted After 14 Days
All student audio recordings are automatically deleted by a scheduled process running every 24 hours.
Transcripts Removed After 30 Days
Spoken transcripts are automatically removed after 30 days. Scores and feedback persist; raw spoken content does not.
Enterprise-Grade Storage
Data is stored on a commercially available cloud platform with encryption at rest and in transit, and security rules consistent with enterprise-level standards.

Section 8 — Where This Is Going

Instructor session notes. Written notes linked to any student attempt — visible only to that instructor, persistent with the performance record.
Progress summaries. AI-generated summary of performance patterns across a session or cohort — identifying consistent gaps and areas of growth.
Performance arc. Score trend across attempts over time — a visible arc of progress rather than isolated data points.
Gap-to-activity recommendations. Based on accumulated performance, the platform identifies persistent gaps and recommends targeted corpus passages to address them.
Full curriculum integration. Frames Practice aligned to a completed discourse frame curriculum. Pronunciation module. Platform becomes the practice engine for a complete ILR 3 instructional system.
